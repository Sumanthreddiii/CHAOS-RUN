CHAOS RUN – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 NOVEMBER 2026

CHAOS RUN is a game made by SUMANTH REDDY ("I", "me"). This page explains what information the game collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What I collect

  • Your email address, which you type in before you play.
  • Your name, if you choose to enter one (it is optional).
  • The date and time you signed up, and the name of the game.
  • Your game progress (which levels you have finished). This is saved on your own device.

I do not collect your location, contacts, photos, payment details or passwords.

Why I collect it

  • To let you play and to keep your progress for your email on your device.
  • To know who is playing the game.
  • To email you about new levels or updates. You can ask me to stop at any time.

Where it is stored

Your progress and sign-in details are stored in your browser or installed app on your device. A copy of your sign-up (email, name, time) is sent to an online form or spreadsheet that only I can access. The game is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record basic technical information such as your IP address when you load the game.

Sharing

I do not sell your information. I do not share it with anyone except the services needed to run the game (the web host and the online spreadsheet or form service). If I add advertising or other services in the future, I will update this page, because those services may collect their own data.

Children

CHAOS RUN is meant for players aged 13 and over. If you are younger, please ask a parent or guardian before entering an email address.

Your choices

  • You can ask me to delete your email and name by writing to the address below.
  • You can remove the progress saved on your device by clearing the game's data in your browser or by uninstalling the app.
  • You can choose not to play if you do not want to share your email.

Changes

If I change how the game handles information, I will update this page and the date at the top.
